# Clip outline

What the Planner looks for and how the Editor cuts. Edit anything below; the bold labels are read literally, so keep them.

## Settings

- **Number of clips:** 5
- **Clip length:** 30-75 seconds
- **Platform:** TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16

## Audience

People scrolling short-form video on their phone, sound on, who haven't seen the full video. They give a clip about two seconds before swiping. Describe your audience here: who they are, what language and dialect they speak, what they already know.

## Clipping tone

Pick moments that work on their own: a surprising fact, a sharp joke, a strong opinion, a moving story, or a clear explanation of something people get wrong. Each clip should make one point and land it.

Avoid clips that depend on something said earlier, inside jokes the audience won't get, or long setups with a weak payoff. Treat sensitive subjects with care.

## Story structure

- **Structure:** hook → setup → payoff
- **Cold open:** yes. If the best line comes late, open on it, then cut back to the setup.
- **Max segments per clip:** 3

The first line must work cold. The last line should land: a punchline, a striking fact, or an open question. Don't end mid-sentence or mid-laugh.

## Editing style

- **Remove pauses longer than:** 0.8 seconds
- **Jump-cut zoom:** yes
- **Transitions:** cut, crossfade, zoom, whip
- **Transition length:** 0.4 seconds
- **Effect intensity:** moderate

Keep it tight and moving. Every cut should earn its place.

Transitions can also be `any`, or names from the effects library (Design panel → Browse the effects): `flash_cut`, `impact_cut`, `glitch_cut`, `blur`, `dip_black`, every ffmpeg transition. Effect intensity is `subtle`, `moderate` or `heavy`: how many effects the editor adds.

## Visual effects

- **Zoom effects:** punch_in, slow_push, ken_burns, zoom_out
- **Ken Burns on archive photos:** yes
- **Reframe for vertical:** follow subject
- **Color grade:** punchy
- **Vignette:** subtle
- **Film grain:** none
- **Glow:** none
- **Letterbox bars:** no
- **Fade in and out:** no
- **Flashback look:** none
- **Effects:** any

Effects are what the editor may put on a clip's timeline: text, graphics, your GIFs and stickers, voice treatments and video effects such as `shake`, `zoom_punch`, `glitch`, `speed_lines` or `big_text`. Write `any`, `none`, or the names you allow.

## Captions style

- **Caption style:** word-by-word karaoke
- **Caption font:** Segoe UI
- **Caption size:** 60
- **Caption position:** lower third
- **Words per caption:** 3
- **Highlight colour:** #FFD23F
- **Emphasis colour:** #FF6B4A

## Title card

- **Hook title:** yes
- **Title duration:** 2.5 seconds
- **Title position:** top

## Music

Music comes from your files in `assets/music` (name them for their mood, like `sad_piano_slow.mp3`), ducked under speech. Add a **Music mood:** line to say which kind fits.

- **Background music:** no
- **Music volume:** low
- **Sound effects:** any
- **Loudness:** -14 LUFS

Sound effects are generated (`whoosh`, `impact`, `riser`, `pop`, `ding`, `heartbeat`…) or your files in `assets/sfx`: `any`, `no`, or the names you allow. Loudness masters every clip; short-form apps play at about -14 LUFS.

## Previous clip attempts

- `clips/history.md`

Every cut is logged in the history file. Fill in how each clip performed after posting; the Planner reads it before every new take.

## Extra notes

Anything else the Planner should know: names to spell a certain way, topics to skip, recurring segments that always clip well.
