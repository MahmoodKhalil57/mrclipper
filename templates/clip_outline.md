# Clip outline

What the Planner looks for and how the Editor cuts. Edit anything below; the bold labels are read literally, so keep them.

## Settings

- **Number of clips:** 5
- **Clip length:** 30-75 seconds
- **Platform:** TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16

## Audience

People scrolling short-form video on their phone, sound on, who haven't seen the full video. They give a clip about two seconds before swiping. Describe your audience here: who they are, what language and dialect they speak, what they already know.

## Clipping tone

Pick moments that work on their own: a surprising fact, a sharp joke, a strong opinion, a moving story, or a clear explanation of something people get wrong. Each clip should make one point and land it.

Avoid clips that depend on something said earlier, inside jokes the audience won't get, or long setups with a weak payoff. Treat sensitive subjects with care.

## Story structure

- **Structure:** hook → setup → payoff
- **Cold open:** yes. If the best line comes late, open on it, then cut back to the setup.
- **Max segments per clip:** 3

The first line must work cold. The last line should land: a punchline, a striking fact, or an open question. Don't end mid-sentence or mid-laugh.

## Editing style

- **Remove pauses longer than:** 0.8 seconds
- **Jump-cut zoom:** yes
- **Transitions:** cut, crossfade, zoom, whip
- **Transition length:** 0.4 seconds

Keep it tight and moving. Every cut should earn its place.

## Visual effects

- **Zoom effects:** punch_in, slow_push, ken_burns, zoom_out
- **Ken Burns on archive photos:** yes
- **Reframe for vertical:** follow subject
- **Color grade:** punchy
- **Vignette:** subtle
- **Film grain:** none
- **Glow:** none
- **Letterbox bars:** no
- **Fade in and out:** no
- **Flashback look:** none

## Captions style

- **Caption style:** word-by-word karaoke
- **Caption font:** Segoe UI
- **Caption size:** 60
- **Caption position:** lower third
- **Words per caption:** 3
- **Highlight colour:** #FFD23F
- **Emphasis colour:** #FF6B4A

## Title card

- **Hook title:** yes
- **Title duration:** 2.5 seconds
- **Title position:** top
